# How Sitrep works — in plain words

> **Last updated:** 2026-10-19 · **Authors:** mridun-gupta & Devin (AI-assisted)

```
public reports        Sitrep                         your screen
──────────────        ─────────────────────────      ──────────
earthquake feeds  →   tidy them into one shape   →   dots on a map,
weather warnings      (what/where/when/how bad/      colored by kind,
military planes       which source said it)          sized by danger
news wires                  ↓
markets              where several sources
                     overlap → "hotspot" rings
                          ↓
                     a short summary — written
                     by rules, or your AI helper
                     (it tells you which)
```

## Three things worth knowing

1. **One shape for everything.** Each feed speaks its own format; Sitrep
   translates them all into the same kind of card — what happened, where,
   when, how serious, who said it. That's what makes mixing them possible.
2. **Overlap is the clue.** One report is a dot. When different sources
   point at the same place, Sitrep draws a ring — that's where attention
   pays off.
3. **Honest about failure.** If a feed dies, its corner dims and the status
   panel shows it. Empty means empty — never guessed.
