# What Sitrep is for — in plain words

> **Last updated:** 2026-10-19 · **Authors:** mridun-gupta & Devin (AI-assisted)

## The idea

Sitrep is a screen that watches the world for you. It gathers public
reports — earthquakes, big storms, disease outbreaks, internet blackouts,
military planes, space-weather warnings and the day's headlines — and puts
them on one map so you can see trouble early.

## The rules it lives by

1. **Nothing is made up.** Every dot and headline links back to the real
   source it came from. If a source goes quiet, the app says so instead of
   filling the gap.
2. **AI is optional and honest.** If you connect an AI helper, it only
   *summarizes* what's already there — it never invents events — and the
   brief says "ai" when it helped. Without it, plain rules write the summary.
3. **Broken things look broken.** When a feed fails, you can see it marked
   "down" in the app's status panel — no silent gaps.
4. **Free is the default.** Everything works without paying anyone.
   A few optional feeds need a free signup key, but nothing basic is locked.

## What it watches

Earthquakes (USGS) · storms and disasters (NASA, UN/EU GDACS) · US weather
alerts (National Weather Service) · military aircraft that are broadcasting
their position (adsb.lol) · internet outages by country (IODA) ·
space-weather warnings (NOAA) · disease outbreaks (WHO) · ten news wires ·
crypto and market moves · plus optional conflict and wildfire feeds.

## What it is not

It can't predict the future, it can't see planes that turn their trackers
off, and "no alerts" on a quiet night is the truth, not a failure.
